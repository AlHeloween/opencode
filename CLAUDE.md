## Compact Instructions

Owner, 2026-10-14 — the principle of the compact (read by Claude Code's built-in compaction; documented route:
code.claude.com/docs/en/how-claude-code-works#when-context-fills-up):

A compact is not an archive of the retold past but the current projection of the recursively linked history of the
inquiry. Every material claim must stay traceable to its primary source, and the projection is recomputed for the
current intention without changing the original evidence.

**Compress the representation, not the evidence.**

Keep the links between the intention, decisions, hypotheses, refutations, check results and unfinished tasks. Separate
the observed from the assumed, checks run from checks planned. Do not invent missing sources or references.

After the context is restored, continue from the last PROVEN state, not from the last convincing retelling.

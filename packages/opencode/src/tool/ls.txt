Directory tree listing. Product `list` replaces shell ls/dir/tree.

Hardcoded ignores (always applied): node_modules/, __pycache__/, .git/, dist/, build/, target/, vendor/, bin/, obj/, .idea/, .vscode/, .zig-cache/, zig-out, .coverage, coverage/, tmp/, temp/, .cache/, cache/, logs/, .venv/, venv/, env/. Gitignore rules are honored in addition. `ignore` adds extra glob patterns.

Output: hierarchical tree, directories suffixed `/`, absolute paths. `directoriesOnly: true` = structure only (tree -d). Cap: 100 entries (truncation notice on overflow).

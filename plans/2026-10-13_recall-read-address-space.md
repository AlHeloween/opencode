## Owner ruling (verbatim, binding)

The 8 000-char automatic threshold goes away; a result is released only when the robot itself releases it (`recall … keep`), by a declared span (`ttl_until`), or — as the one automatic bound left — when it exceeds **16 384 lines OR 256 KB, whichever first** (owner, 2026-10-13, on `recall` of a 10 MB file: «16384 строк или 256кб ограничение, свернет если надо»). That ONE limit (two constants, one home) is both the fold gate and recall's per-call return cap (replacing `REPLAY_TOOL_OUTPUT_MAX_CHARS` = 32 000). DECIDED the same day, on Delphi sources («тут у меня дельфи код, там море классов — если не прочитать весь заголовок, там делать нечего»): `read`'s OWN limits (`DEFAULT_READ_LIMIT` 2 000 and `MAX_BYTES` 50 KB, constants in `tool/read.ts:18,28`) move to the same pair, so one `read` returns a whole unit header. `tool/truncate.ts` (shared by every OTHER tool) stays as it is.
